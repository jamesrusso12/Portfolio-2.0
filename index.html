GIMM Senior Showcase Reel

For my Boise State University senior showcase, I presented my capstone Mixed Reality game built in Unity 6 using the Meta All-in-One SDK. The project combines VR and AR interactions, showcasing dynamic spatial awareness, passthrough environments, and player feedback systems optimized for Quest 3 hardware.

I designed and developed every aspect of the game, from core gameplay logic to UI/UX design, environmental lighting, and comfort features for player experience. This project represents the culmination of my degree and the skills I’ve developed in immersive media development.
