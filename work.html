The challenge

Design a Mixed-Reality ghost-hunting shooter that feels good on Quest 3 hand-tracking — readable HUD, tight shoot feel, and stable performance — in a semester, solo.

What I built

  • Custom debris & impact physics on Unity 6's physics system for destructible environments.
  • Spatial audio + fog VFX pipeline tuned for MR passthrough, so threats are legible by sound alone.
  • Hand-tracking first interaction model — grab thresholds, haptics, spatial reticle — tuned against playtest data.
  • Diegetic HUD designed for MR comfort (no eye-attached text, high-contrast glyphs).

Outcome

  • 72 FPS stable on Quest 3 with full hand-tracking & passthrough
  • Solo-shipped core loop, level, VFX, audio, UI
  • Selected for Boise State senior showcase

Stack: Unity 6 · C# · Meta AIO SDK v72 · Hand Tracking · Passthrough API · Spatial Audio · Blender

The challenge

Partner with Mississippi State's special-needs department to build an iOS AR reader that makes children's books come alive — accessible enough for kids with cognitive and motor differences, and stable on common classroom iPads.

What I owned

  • ARKit pipeline — image recognition, 3D model placement, and real-time tracking against physical book pages.
  • Co-designed the UI with the MSU research team, translating their accessibility research into SwiftUI components.
  • AR scene rendering — loading & positioning book-specific assets at runtime.

Outcome

  • Delivered to MSU partner program for student use
  • Co-built with practicing accessibility researchers
  • Informed my approach to accessible XR ever since

Stack: SwiftUI · ARKit · Xcode · Figma · 3D modeling
