2D Fighter

A fighting-game prototype showcasing AI combat logic, animation Blueprints and Blueprint-driven attack combo systems. Built out of the Unreal University course “Build a Fighting Game in Unreal Engine”.

  • Attack system routing Enhanced Input actions through a blocking check into separate air and ground attack branches.
  • Block system that sets an is-blocking flag and drops max walk speed to zero, restoring it on release.
  • Damage handling that subtracts from player health, updates the health bar, then branches to a death animation or a timed damage stun.
  • Two-player input routing through casts to the player character for move, jump, attack and block.

Target Practice MP

A multiplayer target range powered by Unreal's replication system, with networked scoring, dynamic spawns and hit detection. This is the prototype the demo on my landing page is built from.

  • Target spawner feeding a random index from an array of level target points into SpawnActor on a repeating timer.
  • Hit resolution that spawns an explosion emitter, increments the replicated score, plays a sound and destroys the target.
  • Round handling for targets that are never hit.

Parkour movement

A traversal system covering mantle, vault and wall-run, built on Unreal's character movement component.

Stack: Unreal Engine 5 · Blueprints · Enhanced Input · Paper ZD · Replication · Behaviour Trees
